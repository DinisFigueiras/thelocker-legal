Help for The Locker, the football shirt collection app.

The Locker is an app for your football shirt collection: photograph a shirt, let AI catalogue it, then keep the whole collection in one place with search, filters, stats, backups and cards you can save, copy or send.

Contact us

Something not working, or an idea to make The Locker better? Write to us. We usually reply within 2 business days. Please include your device model, the iOS version and a short description of the problem.

Questions people ask

Where is my collection stored?

Only on your device. The Locker has no accounts and no cloud sync. If you lose or reset your phone, the collection is lost unless you made a backup. Go to Settings → Export a backup regularly and keep the file somewhere safe (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email).

How do I move my collection to a new phone?

On the old phone use Settings → Export a backup and save the file. On the new phone install The Locker, open Settings → Restore from a backup and pick the file. The free plan restores up to 5 shirts; The Locker Pro restores them all.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open Settings, tap the Pro card and choose Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

The AI scan says it needs a break

To keep the AI feature available and affordable, scans are limited to 10 per hour. When you reach the limit, scanning pauses for about 30 minutes: the + button shows an hourglass with a countdown, and a short message tells you when you can scan again. You can still browse and edit your collection while you wait.

The AI got the club, season or condition wrong

AI results are suggestions. Check every field on the review screen before saving, and edit a saved shirt any time with the Edit button on its page.

Where does my photo go when I tap “Catalogue this shirt”?

The photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and on to Google Gemini for analysis. We do not store it. See the Privacy policy for details.

How do I share a shirt?

Open a shirt and tap Unveil it, pick a look by swiping, then choose Save, Copy or Share. Saving asks for permission to add images to your photos; the app cannot read your library. Nothing is sent to us.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Delete all my data to remove every shirt, every photo and your collector level from your device. There is nothing to delete on our servers because there are no accounts. To ask about server-side records linked to your device, email us.
