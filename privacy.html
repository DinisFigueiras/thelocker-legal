1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Information stored only on your device

In Short: your collection never reaches our servers.

The kits you add (club, season, kit type, manufacturer, size, condition, player name, purchase price, acquisition date, care notes), their photos, your currency setting and your free/Pro status cache are stored locally on your device. We have no access to them. If you uninstall the app or use Delete Account, this data is removed from your device.

Information we receive when you use the AI scan

In Short: the photo you choose to analyse is processed by an AI service.

  • Photo. When you tap Analyse with AI, the photo you selected is sent over an encrypted connection to our server function (hosted on Supabase) and from there to Google Gemini, which returns the club, season, kit type, manufacturer, condition and care details. We do not store the photo on our servers.
  • Device identifier and IP address. To prevent abuse and control costs, the app sends a random identifier generated on your device (it is not linked to your name, email or advertising ID) and our server sees your IP address. These are used to count how many scans are made in a given period and are stored in a rate-limiting table.

Payment and purchase information

The Locker Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to verify and manage purchases; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase status. Their privacy notice: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Device permissions

  • Camera. Used only to photograph a kit when you choose to scan one.
  • Photo library. Used only to select a kit photo you choose.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings. We do not access your microphone, contacts or location.

Sensitive information

We do not process sensitive personal information. Please avoid including people in the photos you analyse.

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

In Short: we process your information to provide and secure the Services and to comply with law.

  • To identify your kit from a photo and return the result to you (AI scan).
  • To prevent abuse and keep AI costs under control (rate limiting by device identifier and IP address).
  • To verify and restore your Pro purchase.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

3. WHAT LEGAL BASES DO WE RELY ON TO PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

In Short: we process your information only when we have a valid legal reason.

If you are located in the EU or UK, the GDPR and UK GDPR require us to explain the legal bases we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract. To provide the Services you request, such as the AI scan and your Pro purchase.
  • Legitimate interests. To keep the Services secure and prevent abuse, through rate limiting.
  • Consent. When you choose to analyse a photo, you ask us to send it to our AI provider. You can stop at any time by not using the feature.
  • Legal obligations. Where necessary to comply with the law.

5. DO WE OFFER ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE-BASED PRODUCTS?

In Short: yes, the kit scan uses a third-party AI model.

The kit scan uses Google Gemini. The photo is sent only when you tap Analyse with AI, and the app tells you so before you do. Results can be wrong, so the app lets you review and edit every field before saving. We do not use your photos to train our own models. Google processes the data under its own terms; see https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms.

6. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

In Short: your collection stays on your device; server-side data is short-lived.

  • Collection data: kept on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Photos sent for AI analysis: not stored by us. Google may retain them for a limited period according to its own terms.
  • Rate-limiting records (device identifier, IP address, counters): kept only as long as needed to enforce usage limits.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as required for accounting and legal purposes.

8. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

In Short: depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you may have the right to request access to, correction of, erasure of, restriction of or objection to the processing of your personal information, and the right to data portability. You may also withdraw consent at any time. Because we hold almost no data about you, most requests concern the rate-limiting records tied to your device identifier or IP address. To make a request, email mrkingames00@gmail.com; we will respond within one month. You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in Portugal: CNPD).

Withdrawing consent: stop using the AI scan, or uninstall the app. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already took place.
